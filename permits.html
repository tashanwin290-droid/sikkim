Sikkim Protected Area Permit (PAP) & ILP Guide

Everything you need to know about army check-post permits for Nathula Pass, Gurudongmar Lake, Zero Point, and Old Silk Route.

Government Mandated Border Clearance

Why are Permits Required in Sikkim?

Due to international borders with China (Tibet Autonomous Region), Bhutan, and Nepal, several high-altitude tourist destinations in Sikkim fall under the Protected Area category.

All tourists (Indian & Foreign Nationals) require prior permits issued by the Sikkim Tourism & Civil Aviation Department and endorsed by the Indian Army Check-posts. Sikkim Club handles 100% of the document verification, queueing, and approvals on your behalf.

Nathula Pass PAP (Indo-China Border)
Issued only to Indian Citizens. Closed on Mondays & Tuesdays. Issued max 24 hours in advance by lottery allocation.
North Sikkim PAP (Lachung, Lachen & Gurudongmar)
Required for all Indian tourists. Foreign nationals can visit Lachung & Yumthang Valley with minimum 2 persons in a group with RAP/PAP.
Old Silk Route PAP (Zuluk, Gnathang, Kupup)
Processed through Rongli SDPO check-post for Indian travelers exploring the historic trade route.

Important Sikkim Permit Rules & FAQ

Read these essential tips before packing your bags for Sikkim.

Can foreign nationals visit Nathula Pass?

No. Due to strict military border regulations, foreign passport holders (including OCI/PIO cardholders) are not allowed at Nathu La Pass and Gurudongmar Lake. However, foreigners can visit Tsomgo (Changu) Lake, Lachung, Yumthang Valley, and Pelling with valid permits in a group of 2 or more.

How many days in advance should I apply?

For North Sikkim (Lachung/Lachen), send documents at least 24 hours prior to travel. For Nathula Pass, permits are issued on a first-come, first-served quota basis by Sikkim Tourism 1 day in advance. We recommend booking with Sikkim Club 3 to 7 days before your trip.

Are permits required for children under 5 years?

Yes, children of all ages require permit endorsement. For minors who do not have a Voter ID or Passport, a valid Birth Certificate with photo ID or School ID along with parents' documents is required.

What if Nathula Pass is closed due to snowfall?

If the road beyond 15th Mile is blocked due to heavy snowfall or landslides, cabs visit Tsomgo (Changu) Lake and Baba Mandir instead. Sikkim Club provides transparent adjustments and support during bad weather situations.
