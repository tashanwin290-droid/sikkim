Lachung - Serene Alpine Valley & Gateway to Yumthang

Lachung is a picturesque mountain village in North Sikkim surrounded by snow-dusted peaks, pine forests, and roaring mountain streams. Famous as the base for Yumthang Valley (Valley of Flowers) and Zero Point (Yumesamdong).

Key Highlights & Activities:
  • Yumthang Valley of Flowers & Natural Hot Springs
  • Zero Point (Yumesamdong) at 15,300 ft snow line
  • Scenic wooden cottages and apple orchards
